Demand by district

Which districts need the most water in a given month?

Districts are administrative areas, not water supply zones; figures show expected billed demand, not pipe routing. How are district figures estimated? District forecast = city forecast for that month × the district’s average share of the same calendar month over the last 3 years with data. Chosen by a backtest over 7 years (Sep 2017 – Aug 2024): it beat the 12-month average share and last year’s same-month share. On average about 5% of a month’s water lands in the wrong district. The low–high range reflects city forecast uncertainty only; the district split adds about 5% further error. One loss rate (32.4%) is applied to all districts; real losses vary by district.
